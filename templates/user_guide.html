Access and roles

The home page, the PGC documentation, the CRISP-ML pages and this guide are public. Everything else needs an account. Create one in Register (full name, username, email and a password of at least eight characters with letters and numbers). Every new account starts as a viewer; only an administrator can give a higher role. If you open a protected page without logging in, the system sends you to Log in and then back to the page you asked for.

Viewer

1. Log in. Your role appears next to your name in the menu.
2. Open Operations → Intersections to see the four intersections and their map links.
3. Open Operations → Timing plans to read the plans that were approved or applied.
4. Open Operations → Reports to see the indicators.
5. To send a comment, open Operations → Incidents and feedback → New, choose the intersection and describe it.

Operator: from a forecast to a plan

1. Open Operations → New forecast, choose the intersection, the date and the hour, and press Run forecast. The server runs the model and saves the result with your user and the model version.
2. Read the forecast (vehicles per hour), the type of moment (off-peak, moderate or peak), the curve of the day and the suggested green time.
3. Press Create timing plan. You can move the proposal by up to 15 seconds; a larger change needs a written justification of at least 10 characters. Save the draft.
4. Press Submit for approval. When an administrator approves it, press Mark as applied.
5. After the hour has passed, open the forecast again and record the real vehicle count in Real outcome. The system calculates the error and uses it in the reports and the monitoring.

Administrator

1. Open a pending plan and approve it, or reject it writing the reason (at least 10 characters).
2. Manage intersections from Operations → Intersections: create, edit or disable. An intersection with forecasts or plans cannot be deleted.
3. Open Admin → Users and roles to change a role or disable an account. Nobody can change their own role, and at least one active administrator must remain.
4. Review Incidents and feedback: assign, change the status and close each one with a resolution.
5. Open Admin → Monitoring every week to read the alerts, the live error and the model versions.

How to read the forecast

  • Meaning. The number is the average number of vehicles expected in that hour at that intersection. On the independent test the average error was about 5.6 vehicles per hour.
  • Suggested green time. 8 + 52 × min(1, forecast ÷ 95th percentile of the intersection) seconds, always between 8 and 60. It is a proposal: a person adjusts and approves it.
  • Dates far from the data. The model learned from November 2015 to June 2017. For dates more than 60 days after that, the page shows a warning and the forecast keeps the last level it learned.
  • Less reliable intersections. The forecast is more accurate at intersections 1 and 2 than at 3 and 4.
  • Data. The counts are a public dataset assigned to Chía, not field measurements, so the system demonstrates the process.

Incident reporting and feedback

  1. Any user opens Operations → Incidents and feedback → New, chooses the intersection, the kind (feedback, incident or model issue) and the severity (low, medium or high), and describes what happened. Viewers can send feedback only.
  2. The system registers it as open with the author and the date.
  3. An administrator reviews it, changes it to in review and assigns an operator if needed.
  4. The assignee investigates. If the cause is the model, the forecast is linked and noted for the next retraining.
  5. The administrator or the assignee closes it with a resolution (required); it then appears in the reports.

Incidents are reviewed within one working day. Technical errors are also logged automatically and shown on the monitoring page.
