Artificial Intelligence project

Smart Traffic Light Control with Artificial Intelligence

Four intersections of Chía: forecast the vehicles per hour, turn the forecast into a green-time plan that a person approves, and check it against what really happened.

Read the documentation Open the system Watch the presentation
1 · GroupUnderstand the data and prepare it. K-Means finds what kind of moment each intersection is in.
2 · PredictModel engineering and evaluation: five models compared; Gradient Boosting forecasts the vehicle flow (test R² 0.904).
3 · DecideModel engineering and evaluation: a DQN agent learns when to keep or change the green.
4 · OperateInformation system: roles, forecasts stored with their model version, timing plans approved by a person and monitoring.
