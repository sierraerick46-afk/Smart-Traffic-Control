Process

  1. An operator asks the model for the demand of an intersection at a date and hour.
  2. The system proposes a green time from the forecast.
  3. The operator adjusts it within the limits and submits the plan.
  4. An administrator approves or rejects it.
  5. An approved plan is marked as applied; the real count is recorded later and compared with the forecast.
